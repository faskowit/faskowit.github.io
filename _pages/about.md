Hi there! 👋 I'm a postdoctoral fellow in the [Section on Functional Imaging Methods](https://fim.nimh.nih.gov/) (aka [Dr. Peter Bandettini's group](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-conducted-at-nimh/principal-investigators/peter-bandettini)) at the [NIMH](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/). I study how functional networks are organized across the brain 🧠 in space 📍 and time ⏱️, with a particular interest in edge-centric approaches to fMRI and computational models of brain organization. 

I completed my Ph.D. in 2021 under the supervision of Dr. Olaf Sporns at [Indiana University](https://www.indiana.edu/) and thereafter I completed a postdoc year jointly with the [Brain Networks & Behavior Lab](https://www.brainnetworkslab.com/) and the [Computational Cognitive Neuroscience Lab](http://www.indiana.edu/~cortex/). My work sits within a scientific community that examines brain architecture through the lens of networks 🕸️, using network science & graph theorical applications. An advantage of our network neuroscience approach is that we can attempt to clarify "organization" with intuitive algorithms and simplifying operations 💻, all while retaining sensitivity to the complexities of our data. My grad work was supported by an NSF GRFP, the IU graduate school, and the IU psych department. 

Check out [my Github](https://github.com/faskowit) for scripts, code, and data related to these endeavors ([projects](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-31202-1) on community detection in brain networks [here](https://github.com/faskowit/Faskowitz2018wsbmLifeSpan) and [here](https://github.com/faskowit/Faskowitz2019wsbmRatBrain)! Also, some edgy tools [here](https://github.com/brain-networks/edge-centric_demo) and [here](https://github.com/faskowit/overlap_on_NxN_func) and [here](https://github.com/brain-networks/edge-ts)). I've also been compiling a list of [openly-available brain network data](https://github.com/faskowit/brain-networks-across-the-web), for all of us to enjoy. I've benefited greatly from my colleagues' openly available data and code. Hopefully I can return the favor! 

I studied neuroscience and cognitive science (and minored in marketing 🤷) at the [University of Southern California](https://dornsife.usc.edu/) ✌️ (2014), and spent my time as an undergraduate assistant in Dr. Mara Mather's [Emotion and Cognition Lab](http://gero.usc.edu/labs/matherlab/). Following graduation, I made the trek westward to Marina del Rey, where I worked as a research assistant for the USC [Imaging Genetics Center](http://igc.ini.usc.edu/) under the mentorship of Dr. Neda Jahanshad. There I assisted on a wide range of projects (including [Enigma](http://enigma.ini.usc.edu/)), and picked up an affinity for bash scripting, data sharing, and non-linear registration techniques. 

I'm originally from Orange County, CA - a nice locale with a stunning shoreline 🌴, great weather, and a vibrant sporting culture. I'm an admirer of [modern architecture](http://www.getty.edu/visit/center/architecture.html) and [abstract art](http://www.mwmgraphics.com/vectorfunk.html). I enjoy perusing Google Earth 🌍 for unique geological features and man-made wonders. I am fond of Americano [coffee](https://deansbeans.com/), spy novels, 1970s [prog-rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selling_England_by_the_Pound), Jeopardy!, grillin' burgers, and [Tottenham Hotspur](https://www.reddit.com/r/coys/) Football Club. I'm an especially avid disc golf 🥏⛳️ and pickleball player 🥒🎾. I would be remiss not to mention my wonderful family: my wife Elizabeth and our baby daughter, who shares my affection for [Richard Scarry](https://www.richardscarry.com/) illustrations and long afternoon naps.
